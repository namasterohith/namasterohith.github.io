How I think about leadership.

Good engineering leadership is about making better decisions possible — not just shipping more code.

From product intent to architecture

I enjoy the messy space between a business problem and a technical solution: clarifying the trade-offs, shaping a practical direction and helping teams build systems that can evolve.

Teams that can own outcomes

My approach is to create clarity, sustainable delivery practices and room for engineers to make sound decisions — rather than becoming a bottleneck for every decision.

AI-native, judgment-led

I'm exploring how agentic AI changes software delivery. Faster execution matters, but so do engineering guardrails, architecture, security and accountability for what ships.

Startup pragmatism

Having worked across engineering, product and a co-founder/CTO role, I value speed without losing sight of maintainability, customer needs and business constraints.
